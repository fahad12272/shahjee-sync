Privacy Policy

Shahjee Sync — shahjee-sync.vercel.app

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Shahjee Sync is a social media scheduling web app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it and how you can delete it. We collect the minimum data needed to publish your own content to your own connected accounts.

2. What Data We Collect

DataWhy we collect it
Account details (name, email, password hash)To create and secure your Shahjee Sync login
TikTok basic profile (display name, avatar, username, open_id)To show which TikTok account is connected in your dashboard
TikTok access & refresh tokensTo publish videos to your TikTok account when you ask us to
Your posts (caption, image/video, schedule time)To store and publish the content you create in the app
Basic profile data from other platforms you connect (Facebook, Instagram, X, etc.)Same purpose: display the connection and publish on your request

We do not collect TikTok video history, follower lists, direct messages, or any data about people who view your content.

3. How We Use TikTok Data (Login Kit & Content Posting API)

  • user.info.basic: used only to display your connected TikTok account (name and avatar) inside your dashboard.
  • video.upload / video.publish: used only when you explicitly choose to publish a video you uploaded yourself. The video is sent to the TikTok account that authorized the connection. We never post without your action.
  • Tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and are never shared with anyone.
  • You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from Connected Accounts, which deletes the stored tokens immediately.

4. Third Parties We Share Data With

  • TikTok API: your video and caption are sent to TikTok only when you publish.
  • Cloudinary: your uploaded media is hosted there so it can be delivered to the destination platforms.
  • Meta / X / Telegram / LinkedIn / Threads APIs: content you choose to publish is sent to those platforms.
  • We never sell your data. We never use your content to train models or for advertising.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored in a secured cloud database (MongoDB Atlas) with access controls.
  • Platform access/refresh tokens are encrypted before storage.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never readable.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Deleting a post removes its content and media reference from our database.
  • Disconnecting a platform deletes that platform's tokens immediately.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Delete Account) removes your account, posts and all stored platform tokens.
  • You can also revoke our access from your TikTok app settings (TikTok → Settings → Security and permissions → manage app authorizations).

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change. Material changes will be announced inside the app.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us through the app or at the support address shown inside the Service.
